偏旁部首 — Common Radicals
Radicals are the building blocks that hint at meaning or pronunciation

A radical (部首 bùshǒu) is a graphical component used to categorize Chinese characters in dictionaries. Knowing ~214 radicals helps you guess the meaning of thousands of unfamiliar characters. The radical often appears on the left side (偏旁 piānpáng) of a character.

书写技巧 — Writing Tips
Practical advice for mastering Hanzi writing
Use Grid Paper
Practice on 田字格 (tián zì gé) four-square grid paper to maintain proper proportions and balance.
Trace First
Use tracing sheets (描红 miáo hóng) to build muscle memory before writing independently.
Daily Practice
Write each character 10× daily. Consistency matters more than long infrequent sessions.
Learn by Radicals
Group characters by shared radicals to accelerate vocabulary learning and recall.
